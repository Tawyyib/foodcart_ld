Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering and delivery

How fast is Same-Day Lagos delivery?

Orders placed before 12:00 PM (Monday–Saturday) are delivered same-day across Lagos State. Orders after 12:00 PM ship first thing the following morning.

How do I qualify for Wholesale & Stockist pricing?

Simply register for a B2B Onboarding account. Once verified, factory rates automatically replace retail prices whenever you are logged in!

What guarantees freshness and quality?

All Nutragold® staples are sourced directly from certified farms and processed in sealed, hygienic food-grade bags to guarantee stone-free purity.
